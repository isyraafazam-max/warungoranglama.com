Company Case Study

Real-World Example

Warung Orang Lama is a local F&B outlet opened in early 2024 and started by Hairol Izad Redzuan, Nathilah Mohd. Said and their business partner Sarah Rashid. It began by selling ready-to-eat sambal ikan bilis under the brand Sambal Bilis Orang Lama, then grew into a café serving traditional Malaysian food such as nasi lemak, lempeng, roti bakar, mee goreng, asam pedas and fried chicken.

It targets locals, families, students and tourists around Nilai, and mixes traditional food with a modern café theme to create a unique identity.

IT Used and Its Contribution

The café uses Loyverse POS for orders, payments, stock and sales reports. It has made daily work faster, reduced errors, supported better decisions from real sales data and improved the customer experience, so staff can focus on quality food and service.
