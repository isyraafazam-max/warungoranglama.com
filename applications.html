IT Applications

Three IT Applications

These are the main IT applications used in a digitalized food business like Warung Orang Lama.

1

Ordering System

Orders are keyed into the POS by staff, which reduces mistakes. Customers can also order online through platforms such as foodpanda or ShopeeFood, which helps the café reach new customers.

2

Digital Payment System

Customers can pay by QR, e-wallet, online banking or card. Each payment is recorded automatically, so checkout is faster and cash handling is reduced.

3

Inventory System

Shows how much of each ingredient or product is left, so the café knows when to restock, avoids running out of popular items and avoids buying too much.

IT ApplicationExplanation
Ordering SystemAllows customers to order food online through websites or apps such as foodpanda or ShopeeFood.
Digital Payment SystemEnables customers to pay using e-wallets, online banking or cards.
Inventory SystemHelps businesses manage product availability.

Featured Tool: Loyverse POS

Warung Orang Lama uses Loyverse POS (Point of Sale) to keep orders, payments, stock and sales records in one digital place.

How it works: staff choose the food and drinks the customer ordered, the system adds up the total, and once the customer pays the transaction is saved. Management can check past transactions, monitor stock and open sales reports showing the best-selling items and the busiest days.

How it is used: the café uses real sales data to decide what to stock, when to offer promotions and whether to change prices or the menu.
