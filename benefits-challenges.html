Benefits and Challenges

Benefits

Productivity

Orders are typed in quickly and totals are automatic, so staff serve more customers at busy times and have more time for food and service.

Efficiency

Less manual calculation, fewer errors and better stock planning reduce wasted money.

Customer Experience

Quicker service, clear totals and easy cashless payment mean less waiting.

Better Decisions

Sales reports show popular items and busy days to guide stock, staffing, promotions and menu.

Challenges and Solutions

Internet dependency

Challenge: slow or lost internet delays payments, online orders and access to reports, and a reliable connection costs money each month.
Solution: keep a backup plan (notebook orders, mobile data) and train staff on which POS functions work offline.

Technical problems

Challenge: device crashes, software errors and failed payments slow service and can add repair costs.
Solution: maintain devices, update software, keep a backup device, back up sales data and train staff on simple fixes.

Digital skills requirements

Challenge: staff have different skill levels, so new employees may make errors or slow the team.
Solution: proper training, simple guides near the cashier, help from experienced staff and ongoing practice.

Conclusion

Information Technology helps Warung Orang Lama improve productivity, efficiency and customer experience while keeping its traditional character. The challenges (internet reliance, technical faults and digital skills) are real but can be managed with backup plans, maintenance and training. Used well, IT lets a small food business stay competitive and succeed.
