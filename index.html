Introduction

Information Technology (IT) is now part of everyday business, from digital payments to online ordering. This website looks at how a small food business, Warung Orang Lama in Putra Nilai, Negeri Sembilan, uses IT to run its café while keeping its traditional Malaysian identity.

We explain the café’s background, the IT applications it relies on (mainly the Loyverse POS), a real-world case study, and the benefits and challenges of using IT in a small food business.
