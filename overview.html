Topic Overview

Background Information

Warung Orang Lama is a local café at Putra Nilai, Bandar Baru Nilai, Negeri Sembilan, opened in early 2024. It specialises in traditional Malaysian food such as nasi lemak, lempeng, roti bakar, mee goreng and fried chicken, with local drinks like Teh O and coffee. Its old-world theme, with vintage décor, traditional furniture and classic P. Ramlee songs, lets customers feel what it was like to dine at an old Malaysian warung.

The café is a good place for families, students, friends and locals. It helps preserve Malaysian food and culture at a time when fast food and modern cafés are popular, teaches younger generations about traditional dishes, creates jobs and buys from local suppliers.

Small Business Digitalization

Digitalization means using technology to run daily operations more efficiently. Warung Orang Lama combines tradition with technology: it uses a Point of Sale (POS) to take orders, calculate totals, record payments, track stock and view sales reports. This keeps its unique brand while making the business faster, more accurate and more competitive.
